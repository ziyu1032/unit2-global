Two Chinese regions, two open-economy models. Hainan is a tropical island building a world-class Free Trade Port. Xinjiang is a land-border region piloting a Free Trade Zone. Today you will master both — and talk about them in English!

🌴 Hainan FTP

Mission: island industries — tourism, services, high-tech.

Weapons: zero-tariff list, 15% income tax, simplified visa.

Style: largest FTP by land area. 🌊

⛰️ Xinjiang Pilot FTZ

Mission: border industries — trade, logistics, energy.

Weapons: land ports, Central Asia connectivity, customs supervision.

Style: China's western gateway. 🚂

🌴 Hainan FTP

Strategic Priorities: Tropical tourism, modern services, high-tech industries, high-efficiency tropical agriculture.

Industrial Clusters: Digital economy, commercial aerospace, deep-sea tech, modern biomedicine, petrochemical new materials.

Policy Advantages: Zero-tariff list, 15% corporate & personal income tax, simplified visa, largest FTP by land area.

⛰️ Xinjiang Pilot FTZ

Strategic Priorities: Border trade, logistics hub, energy processing, modern agriculture, textile industry.

Industrial Clusters: Cross-border e-commerce, energy & chemical processing, textile & garment, agricultural product export.

Policy Advantages: Border trade facilitation, land port advantages, Central Asia connectivity, special customs supervision.

🌴 Hainan — an island province in the South China Sea. Sea routes, tourists, and a huge open-economy experiment.
⛰️ Xinjiang — China's northwestern border region. It connects China to Central Asia by land ports.
